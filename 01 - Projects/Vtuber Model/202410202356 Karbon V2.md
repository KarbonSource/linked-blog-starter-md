There appears to be an issue when I made the eye physics where parts of the eye would clip through, so I skipped making eye physics. I will most likely try again and see if I can make it work.

# Hair Strands separated
![[202410202356 Karbon V2-3.png]]
The hair strands should be separated next time, though this means that setting up hair physics will take more work

# Shadows
## Face shadow and neck shadow color
![[202410202356 Karbon V2-4.png]]
It would be wise to make the shadow of the face and neck the same color to make it more seamless.

## separate back hair shadow

I'm not happy with how the shadow of the back hair turned out in certain head angles, I would to separate the shadow from the back hair so I can edit it if I need to.

## Hair shadows for detail
![[202410202356 Karbon V2-6.png]]
Hair shadows for the bangs should be added to give more details and depth, once again it should be separated from everything else.
