# １　これ　それ　あれ　どれ
when we wanna talk about things we don't know the names of, we use "thing thing" "that one" and so forth. In Japanese, we use *kore*, *sore*, and *are*.
	これはいくらですか　　　*How much is this?*
	それはさんぜんえんです　　　That is 3000 yen
*Kore* refers to a thing that is close to you, the speaker. *Sore* is something that is close to the person you are talking to, and *are* refers to a thing that is neither close to the speaker nor the listener.

*dore* is an expression for which, for example
	どれですか　　　which one is it

Questions words like *dore* and *nani* cannot be followed by the particle *wa*. Instead, you must use the particle *ga.*

# 2 この／その／あの／どの + noun
A slightly more specific *kore*, *sore*, *are*, and *dore* is *kono*, *sono*, *ano*, and *dono* together with a noun. (Note that the *re* series must always stand alone, while the *no* series must always be followed by a noun.) Thus, if you know that the item in your hand is a watch

instead これはいくらいですか　-> you can say このとけいはいくらいですか
*TL: how much is this? -> How much is this watch?*

Similarly if you are talking about a watch that is held by the person you are talking to, you can say:
そのとけいはさんぜんえんです
*TL: That watch is 3000 yen*

And if the watch is far from both the speaker and the listener
あのとけいはさんぜんごひゃくえんです
*TL: That watch over there cost 3500 Yen*

If you know one of several watches cost 3500 yen but don't know which one then you can say:
どのとけいがさんぜんごひゃくえんです
*TL: Which watch cost 3500 Yen?*

Since *dono* is a question word, just like *dore*, we cannot use the particle *wa* with it; we must use *ga*

# 3 ここ　そこ　あそこ　どこ
*koko*, *soko*, *asoko*, *doko* are used for places.
![[202610211645 Lesson 2; Shopping-1.png]]

you can ask for directions by saying:
すみません。ゆうびんきょくはどこですか。
*TL: Excuse me. Where is the post office?*

If you are close by, you can point toward the post office and say:
(ゆうびんきょくは) あそこです。
*TL: (Post office) right over there.*

# 4 だれの noun
We now learn how to ask who something belongs to. The question for the word "who" is *dare*, and for "whose", we simply add the particle *no*.

これは<u>だれの</u>かばんですか。　　それは<u>スーさんの</u>かばんです
*TL: <u>Whose</u> bag is this?     This is <u>Sue's</u> bag.*

# 5 noun も
In lesson 1, we learned how to say "Item A is this, Item B is that." We now learn how to say "Item A is this, and Item B is this, too."

たけしさんはにほんじんです。
*Takeshi is a Japanese person*

みちこさんもにほんじんです。
*Michiko is Japanese, <u>too</u>*
