Kitty O'Neil was a race care driver, daredevil and stunt legend while also being deaf. She was known as being fearless.

She accumulated several diseases that ultimately destroyed her hearing. She was discouraged from using sign language by her mother.

She became one of the first women to sign with the leading stunt agency in Hollywood.

There was a negative view of people using sign language at the time, so she was an expert at reading lips.

In 1976 she became the fastest woman in the world
