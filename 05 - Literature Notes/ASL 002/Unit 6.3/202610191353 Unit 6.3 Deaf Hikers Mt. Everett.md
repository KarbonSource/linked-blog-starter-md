Scott and Shayna were stuck at the Everest Base Camp for nearly two week waiting. This was due to weather conditions, since Everest experienced an exceptionally cold season that year. With an average wind speed of 20-30 mph and temps from -30 to -50 degrees F at the summit.

The primary concern revolved around the jet stream. Due to the high winds, the fixed rope team couldn't finish up their task. With the fixed rope setup after 2 weeks many climbers seized the opportunity of climbing the first summit window. During this first wave aprox. 80-80% of climbers headed out towards the summit.
