# Code of professional conduct (CPC)
https://interpreterresource.com/interpreter-education/nad-rid-code-of-professional-conduct-for-sign-language-interpreters/
The CPC is comprised of the following seven tenets:
1. Confidentiality
2. Professional Skills and Knowledge
3. Appropriate conduct
4. Respect for for consumers
5. Respect for colleagues, interns, and students
6. Ethical business practices
7. Professional developments
