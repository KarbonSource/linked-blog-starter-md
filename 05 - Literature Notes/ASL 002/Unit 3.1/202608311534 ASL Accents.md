ASL does have accents.

For vocal languages accent is a distinctive way of speaking that sounds different. The way it sounds can indicate where a person comes from or what their language community is.

Sign languages are used in different places and communities too. They have distinctive ways of signing that look different. That's what "accent" is for in sign languages.

An example is that New Yorkers have a reputation for signing fast. Other aspects of you social identity can affect your accent in sign language.

Another example, you age. Sign can look different depending on whether you're older or younger
