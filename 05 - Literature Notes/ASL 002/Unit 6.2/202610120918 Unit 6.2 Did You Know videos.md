# William Ellsworth "Dummy" Hoy was one of the first deaf major league baseball players.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rpMppKC3lRw
William Ellsworth is a baseball who hit .288 during his 14 major league seasons. Hit .300 3 times during his career, scored 100 runs 8 times and produced a .400 on base percentage, 4 times

he set rookie records for games, at bats, singles and walks and was considered flyhawk in his time

He had uncanny speed and stole 82 bases in his rookie year. With a career high of 597 steals, which still ranks high today

In one game, he threw out three runners at home plate from centerfield without an assist

Some say he brought hand signals to baseballs
