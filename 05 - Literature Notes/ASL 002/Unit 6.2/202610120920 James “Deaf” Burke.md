## We changed the game
In 1894, the Gallaudet football team was playing against another deaf team. Paul Hubbard, didn't want the the other teams see him use ASL so to communicate with this team they formed a circle now known as the huddle.

## Today, we're changing the world
For over 150 years, Gallaudet has provided a unique educational experience for deaf/hard of hearing and hearing people. 

## Outcomes
More than 95 percent of recent graduates are employed or in graduate school. 21,000+ alumni are making a difference throughout the world.
