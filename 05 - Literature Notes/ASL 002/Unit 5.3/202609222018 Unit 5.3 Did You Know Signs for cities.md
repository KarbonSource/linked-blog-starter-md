# 202609222018
Names in ASL are Generally fingerspelled. However, many large well known cities have a sign. For example, San Francisco, New York City, Los Angeles, Seattle, Denver, Orlando, etc. There are some cities that have the same exact sign. Like Berkeley and Boston, Boulder and Baltimore. One would only know which city is being referred to by having context of the dialogue.

Smaller cities may also have signs, but they are not well known to the large signing public. For example, if I visit New York City and use the sign for Riverbank, they most likely would not recognize such a small town sign name. To clarify, I would need to fingerspell.

Some cities don't have signs and are just fingerspelled.
