**Will a deaf parent have a deaf baby?**
There is a wide variation in the causes of deafness; because of this, 9 out of 10 deaf children are born to hearing parents, and 1 out of 10 children are born to deaf parents are also deaf. With current tests, it is only possible to determine the probable cause of their deafness for about half of children

**How do deaf mothers hear their babies?**
Most deaf parents prefer to use a vibrating monitor and/or a camera baby monitor. In the deaf community, a baby monitor can be connected to their vibrating alarm clock, which will vibrate the bed when deaf parents sleep.

**Can two deaf parents have a hearing baby**
Globally it is suggested that 90% of people with audiological deafness who have children have hearing children. International studies conducted on hearing children of deaf parents show that these children are raised in families with unique dynamics about hearing children born to hearing parents

Deaf people think in terms of their "inner voice" some believe in ASL, while others think in the vocal language they learned, with their brains coming up with how the vocal language sounds.

**Can a deaf person hear in their dream**
Deaf people experience similar situations as blind people, but their dreams tend to capitalize on sight instead of sound and the other senses. Unless a person can experience hearing within their living memory, it is unlikely to have auditory sensations in their dreams.
