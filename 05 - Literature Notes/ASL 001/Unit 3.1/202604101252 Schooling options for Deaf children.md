# Residential School for the Deaf
students live on campus Monday through Friday. Take the school bus home for the weekend on Friday and return Sunday night by school bus. Day students get transported to and from the residential school by bus. Everyone uses ASL. There is complete access to communication.

# Mainstream at a public school
A deaf student or group of deaf students is placed in a general education class for a percentage of the day. the other percentage of their day is with a teacher of the Deaf in a Special Day Class. In mainstream classroom, students can be pushed in with an interpreter and/or HAT (hearing assistive technology). The HAT is provided at no cost to the student. However, the student's personal hearing aid and/or cochlear implants is their personal property that they pay for themselves.

# Special Day Class (SDC) at a public school
This classroom is for Deaf students who will receive all instruction from a teacher of the Deaf with the support of the classroom signing aides.
