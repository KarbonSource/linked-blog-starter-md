# 202604101323
LIGHT-GREEN Signer signs GREEN in a higher sign space with eyes wide open.  
DARK-GREEN Signer signs GREEN in a lower sign space with eyes squinted.

For very bright colors, pop your eyes open while signing the color with one movement.
