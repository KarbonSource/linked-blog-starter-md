# 202603161627
Hearing dogs are very sound aware, they notice everything that happens in the environment

Hearing dogs' job is to alert their client to sounds going off in the environment. For example, someone knocking at the door, fire alarm, clock

Service dogs may be aware of sounds, but wait for commands from their clients
