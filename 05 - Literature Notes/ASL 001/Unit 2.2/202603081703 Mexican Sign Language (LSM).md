Lengua de senas mexicana (Mexico sign language) short is LSM

Most parts of Mexico they sign the same, there are a few different sings similar to accents

LSM is an official sign language and was recognized in 2005, the deaf community continues to fight for rights to communicate in LSM

LSM has books, videos online, etc. it is better to learn LSM from deaf teachers directly
